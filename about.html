About

Minimal systems, thoughtful structure, and a strong point of view.

I’m Kirstein Genzen Nojapa, a designer and builder focused on simple digital systems that feel refined and function clearly. My work sits between web design, automation, and conversion-focused strategy.

My foundation began in front-end work and early WordPress builds, where I learned how structure, spacing, and typography shape user trust. That evolved into landing pages, CRM builds, and automation workflows built to support businesses rather than clutter them.

Today, I work across the full loop: visual direction, page design, funnel structure, and automation logic. The goal is always the same: reduce noise, keep the customer journey clear, and make important actions feel effortless.

Alongside client and freelance work, I continue building my technical foundation in computer science and systems thinking, which helps me design processes that are not only attractive but sustainable in real business operations.
